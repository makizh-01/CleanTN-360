1. Executive Summary & State Vision

CleanTN 360 (தூய தமிழ்நாடு 360) is a unified smart waste management, AI circular economy, and Green Money digital wallet ecosystem custom-architected for the Government and Citizens of Tamil Nadu. Designed to scale seamlessly across all 38 Revenue Districts, it transforms municipal waste from an urban liability into traceable civic wealth.

Civic Participation & Rewards Citizen Focus

Citizens segregate household and commercial dry waste, deposit it at certified neighborhood hubs, and receive instant, calibrated Green Money points redeemable via UPI or utility bills.

Physical Verification Queue Anti-Fraud Safeguard

Trained municipal collectors physically weigh waste on calibrated digital scales, record tare weights, and capture timestamped scale photographs before ledger approval.

38-District GIS Mapping Statewide Scale

Comprehensive vector GIS map covering all 38 districts with live collection hub densities, segregation indices, and landfill diversion KPIs adhering to CPCB standards.

AI & IoT Infrastructure Phase 5 & 6 Tech

Includes AI visual segregation guidance, predictive seasonal waste forecasting, automated collector route optimization, live GPS fleet tracking, and smart-bin ultrasonic sensors.
